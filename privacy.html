Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

YT Studio Auto ("the app") is a personal tool operated by a single creator to publish content to accounts that the same creator owns (The Worn Wallet and Midnight Americana).

Data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, TikTok, Meta or Pinterest, the app receives an access token that lets it upload videos and images, set titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, manage playlists and schedule posts on the owner's own accounts, and read basic channel/account information and performance statistics.

How data is used and stored

  • Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to perform the publishing actions described above.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect data from viewers or from any other users.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected account's security settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). To request deletion of any stored data, email u20300102@gmail.com; locally stored tokens are deleted within 7 days.
