YT Studio Auto

YT Studio Auto is a private publishing tool used by one independent creator to publish their own videos to their own accounts. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Channels published with this tool:

  • The Worn Wallet — animated personal finance stories (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook)
  • Midnight Americana — American history stories for sleep (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest)
  • Marine Hero Stories — dramatized kindness stories (YouTube)
  • History For Americana — American history stories for sleep (YouTube)

The tool uploads finished videos, titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, and schedules posts, using the official APIs of YouTube, TikTok, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Pinterest.
